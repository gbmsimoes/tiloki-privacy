# Tiloki privacy policy

This public repository will host the privacy policy for **Tiloki**, an offline
Android puzzle game.

`PRIVACY_POLICY.md` is a draft. It cannot become the effective policy until the
publisher's legal name and a monitored privacy/support email are added. Do not
enable GitHub Pages or submit its URL to Google Play until then.

When finalized, the policy will be published at this repository's GitHub Pages
URL and that URL will be added to Tiloki's Google Play listing.

# Tiloki Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** First ads-enabled public Google Play release

Tiloki is an offline puzzle game. This policy applies to ads-enabled Android
releases of **Tiloki** distributed through Google Play.

## Game information stored on the device

Tiloki stores game information on the device only. This includes game progress,
settings such as reduced motion and sound, Daily Challenge records, and
aggregate local QA counters (for example, completed runs, elapsed active time,
move counts, rejected board taps, and pause counts).

Tiloki does not create accounts or send this game information to the publisher
or another party. It does not include cloud saves, a purchase SDK, or a
publisher-run analytics service.

## Advertising data

Tiloki uses **Google AdMob** to serve ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and
process the following information when ads are requested or displayed:

- IP address, which can be used to estimate a device's general location;
- device and advertising identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and
  app set ID where available;
- ad and product interaction data, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions,
  and video views; and
- diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate,
  and energy usage.

Google uses this information to provide advertising, measure and report ad
performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve its services. Google
encrypts data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK in transit. Google's handling of
this information is also governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Personalised advertising and your choices

Tiloki may show personalised ads only where the user has given the required
consent. Where consent is not given, withdrawn, or otherwise required by law,
Tiloki will request non-personalised or limited ads instead.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland,
Tiloki presents a consent message before using personal data for ad
personalisation where required. Users can review or change their advertising
privacy choices from the app's privacy options. Android users can also reset or
delete their advertising ID in their device settings.

## Sharing and retention

Tiloki shares the advertising data described above with Google AdMob. Tiloki
does not use advertising mediation partners. Locally stored game information
remains on the device until the user clears app data or uninstalls the app,
subject to the device's operating-system behavior. The app does not operate a
server-side account or database from which a user must request deletion.

## Security

Tiloki keeps its described game records in the app's local storage. Advertising
data is transmitted to Google using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Users should use
the normal device lock, operating-system updates, and app-store installation
protections appropriate to their device.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a new advertising partner, purchases, analytics,
online features, permissions, or another third-party SDK, this policy and the
Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is
published. Where a change needs consent or a prominent disclosure, the app will
provide it before the relevant data handling begins.

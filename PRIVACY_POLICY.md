# Tiloki Privacy Policy — Draft

**Status:** Draft — not yet effective

**Effective date:** The date of the Google Play release

Tiloki is an offline puzzle game. This policy applies to the no-ad,
no-purchase Android V1 build identified in the Play listing as **Tiloki**.

## Data the V1 build handles

The V1 build stores game information on the device only. This includes game
progress, settings such as reduced motion and sound, Daily Challenge records,
and aggregate local QA counters (for example, completed runs, elapsed active
time, move counts, rejected board taps, and pause counts).

The V1 build does not create accounts and does not send this information to the
publisher or another party. It does not include a third-party analytics SDK,
cloud save, production ad SDK, purchase SDK, or production ad identifier.
Optional historical Daily unlocks remain unavailable when the native rewarded
ad adapter is absent; no live ad is requested by this release configuration.

## Sharing and retention

Tiloki does not share the locally stored game information with third parties in
the V1 build. Data remains on the device until the user clears app data or
uninstalls the app, subject to the device's operating-system behavior. The app
does not operate a server-side account or database from which a user must
request deletion.

## Security

The V1 build keeps the described game records in the app's local storage. The
app does not transmit those records. Users should use the normal device lock,
operating-system updates, and app-store installation protections appropriate to
their device.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds ads, purchases, analytics, online features,
permissions, or another third-party SDK, this policy and the Google Play Data
safety declaration will be updated before that version is published. Where a
change needs consent or a prominent disclosure, the app will provide it before
the relevant data handling begins.
